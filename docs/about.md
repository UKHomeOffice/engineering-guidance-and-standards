The Home Office Engineering Guidance and Standards site is a collection of knowledge for engineers working in the Home Office, across government and the public sector.

This content is created and curated by the Home Office engineering community. The project as a whole is led by the Home Office Digital Engineering Profession team.

It consists of principles, patterns and standards that define how we as engineers work at the Home Office, and how we want to work as engineers in the future.

## What SEGAS does

The Home Office has a lot of teams who work on a lot of technology products. This means we have large amounts of experience and good practice that we can all draw on to help standardise some of the things we do and manage quality consistently.

This experience helps us to deliver technology that is secure, reliable and meets user needs.

In Home Office Engineering, we have communities of practice where people can meet together, share knowledge, ideas and experience. We call these communities guilds.

Guilds are open to anyone in the Home Office who are interested in the subject areas they discuss. Practitioners who lead engineering projects and casual, interested observers from other areas of the organisation are all welcome in our guilds.

## How you can contribute content

All content that we publish on SEGAS follows a content standard. Read our content standard to learn about our process and begin to contribute.

## Why we created the SEGAS site

In 2021, we conducted a discovery across a wide range of our engineering teams, including suppliers and civil servants, and across all our delivery portfolios. We found that there were a number of key areas that we should prioritise for centralised guidance. Since then, our guilds and other working groups have helped to identify the specific needs of our engineers, and develop guidance to meet those needs.

Our guidance is published on GOV.UK to make sure it is:

* transparent about our work; we are committed to following the government [Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard) and the [Home Office 2030 Digital Strategy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-office-2030-digital-strategy/home-office-2030-digital-strategy) by working in the open

* easy for people to find and use; we want any who wants to access our content to be able to quickly discover the information they need

 * a clear source of information for government wider technology communities to show how and why we work, to let us contribute to the wider industry conversation

## Our SEGAS site roadmap

The SEGAS site and contents are always changing and developing. Over time, we intend to:

- increase the number of standards, principles and patterns we host by working with the engineering community
- build a self-assurance mechanism so that teams can easily assert compliance with our standards
- continue developing the site to make sure it stays a good example of how to follow our own standards and guidance
